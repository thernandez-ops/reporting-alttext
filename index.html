Method & sources

Sources: GA4 (alttext.ai · 343732389), Google Ads (2118383642), Meta Ads (act_732204668785430), ChatGPT Ads sheet — via Ryze AI; HubSpot CRM (portal 47927567) direct. Daily data May 29 – Jul 27, 2026. Trials & purchases use the max-reporter rule: per channel per day we take the highest of GA4, Meta and Google Ads. Direct redistribution is an adjustable modeling assumption, not measured truth; a geo/holdout lift test is the way to confirm true incrementality. Recent-day purchases may still be maturing. Deltas compare the selected range with the equal-length window immediately before it. Influence = Pearson correlation of a channel's daily visits/new-users with daily total trials/purchases over the range (needs ≥5 days; correlation ≠ causation).
