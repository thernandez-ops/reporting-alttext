Method & sources

Sources: GA4 (alttext.ai · 343732389), Google Ads (2118383642), Meta Ads (act_732204668785430), ChatGPT Ads sheet — via Ryze AI. Daily data May 21 – Jul 19, 2026. Trials = GA4 sign_up, Purchases = GA4 purchase. Deltas compare the selected range with the equal-length window immediately before it. Influence = Pearson correlation of a channel's daily visits/new-users with daily total trials/purchases over the range (needs ≥5 days; correlation ≠ causation). The Google non-brand pause (Jul 14) is a natural experiment: paid spend −67% while trials rose — evidence that spend was largely non-incremental. Direct redistribution is an adjustable modeling assumption, not measured truth; a geo/holdout lift test is the way to confirm true incrementality. ChatGPT spend spread evenly across Jul 15–19. Recent-day purchases may still be maturing.
