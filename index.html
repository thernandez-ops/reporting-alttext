Method & sources

Sources: GA4 (alttext.ai · 343732389), Google Ads (2118383642), Meta Ads (act_732204668785430), ChatGPT Ads sheet, LinkedIn Ads (514381628) — via Ryze AI; HubSpot CRM (portal 47927567) direct. Daily data Jun 6 – Aug 4, 2026. Trials & purchases use the max-reporter rule: per channel per day we take the highest of GA4, Meta and Google Ads. Direct redistribution is an adjustable modeling assumption, not measured truth; a geo/holdout lift test is the way to confirm true incrementality. Recent-day purchases may still be maturing. HubSpot purchases are counted on the date the sale happened (property hs_v2_date_entered_customer); days above 3x the 60-day median are CRM bulk imports and are replaced with the local median. The 'Channels the paid mix misses' section is GA4 session data by source/medium; LinkedIn and Bing cost is not included in paid spend because those accounts do not report cost through the current connection. Microsoft (Bing) Ads is not connected to Ryze. Search Console has a ~1-day reporting lag, so Aug 4 organic is blank. Deltas compare the selected range with the equal-length window immediately before it. Influence = Pearson correlation of a channel's daily visits/new-users with daily total trials/purchases over the range (needs ≥5 days; correlation ≠ causation). The 'Deep dive' section is a fixed 14-day window (Jul 22 – Aug 4, 2026) and does not move with the date picker, because ad-platform breakdowns (device, placement) are only comparable on a fixed window; Google Ads campaigns there are last 30 days. Meta device/placement figures are Meta-reported; Google Ads figures use all_conversions; landing pages and device visits are GA4; country and device click rates are Search Console; deal stages are HubSpot.
